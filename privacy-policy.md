# Privacy Policy for Qorvynexa

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

Qorvynexa is a subscription-tracking app published under the name **Qorvynexa**. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## 1. Information you enter

Subscription names, prices, billing cycles, renewal dates, currency preferences, reminder settings, and related app settings are stored locally on your device. Qorvynexa does not operate an account system or its own server that receives this information. Your subscription records are not uploaded to Qorvynexa.

## 2. Diagnostics

The app may keep diagnostic information about app errors locally on your device to help you troubleshoot problems. This information is not automatically sent to Qorvynexa. If you choose to copy, export, or share diagnostic information, the information you share may be received by the destination you select.

## 3. Service catalog

The app includes a built-in service catalog and can work offline. When a newer catalog is available, the app may request the public catalog file from:

[Qorvynexa catalog on GitHub Pages](https://nawar79954.github.io/qorvynexa-catalog/catalog.json)

The request fetches the public catalog only; it does not include your saved subscriptions. As with any network request, GitHub may receive technical information such as your IP address and request details. The app checks for catalog updates at most once every 24 hours and only uses a valid catalog with a higher version. If the download fails, the app continues using the available local or cached catalog.

## 4. Advertising

The app may display ads provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, app and ad interaction information, and other data as described in Google's policies and the consent choices available in your region. Ad availability and personalization may depend on your device, region, and consent settings.

Learn more: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. In-app purchases

The app may offer a purchase to remove ads. Purchases are handled by the relevant app store (Google Play or Apple App Store), subject to that store's terms and privacy policy. Qorvynexa does not receive your full payment-card details. Purchase availability and records may be managed by the store and by the app on your device.

## 6. Notifications

If you enable reminders, notification schedules are created locally on your device. The app does not send your reminder schedule to a Qorvynexa server. Your operating system may process notifications as part of providing this feature.

## 7. Backup, export, and sharing

If you use backup, restore, export, or sharing features, the files or information you choose to export may contain subscription details. The data may be shared with an app, service, or person you select. Review the destination and the contents before sharing. Qorvynexa does not control how a third-party destination handles information you choose to send to it.

## 8. App lock and biometrics

If you enable the app lock, a salted hash of your PIN is stored on your device; the PIN itself is not stored as plain text. Fingerprint or face authentication is handled by your device's operating system, and the app does not receive your biometric data. The app lock is an access-control feature and does not encrypt all app data or replace device security.

## 9. Data retention and deletion

Your subscription records and settings remain on your device until you delete them in the app or remove the app and its associated data, subject to your device's backup settings. Exported files and copies shared with other apps or services must be managed separately. Google and app-store providers may retain information according to their own policies.

## 10. Third-party services and links

The app may connect to Google Mobile Ads, Google Play or Apple App Store purchase services, and the public GitHub Pages catalog described above. The app may also open third-party subscription-management or cancellation pages at your request. Those services and websites have their own privacy policies and practices; Qorvynexa does not control them.

## 11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision. Please review this page periodically.
